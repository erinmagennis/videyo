# Videyo logo spec

Status: typography and color tokens recorded. Final yellow play geometry remains taste-blocked until Erin approves a standalone render.

## Wordmark

- Text: `videyo`
- Font family: `Helvetica Neue`, fallback `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Weight: `720` in the wordmark CSS and `800` for the original Yo mark treatment
- Letter spacing: `-0.05em` in CSS; approximately `-18px` at the 280px SVG reference size
- The `yo` letters use Videyo's original Helvetica Neue treatment. The yellow shape changes, not the type treatment.
- The yellow play shape is resized around the fixed `yo` word. Do not shrink or substitute the typography.
- `yo` must be fully contained and vertically centered inside the yellow shape.

## Color tokens

- Yellow play: `#F3D75F` (`--sun`)
- Dark ink: `#18201C` (`--ink`)
- App background: `#121513` (`--bg`)
- Text white: `#F7F4ED` (`--text`)

## Shape direction

- Right-facing play button.
- Rounded corners and a friendly silhouette consistent with the rounded interface.
- The yellow shape fits closely around the full `yo` word with a small, even margin.
- It is one coherent lockup, not a large triangle with small text floating inside.
